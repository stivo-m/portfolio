  (future) A rebuilt personal project — something with visible git history from day one, e.g. a small Go service or a re-imagined version of your old Java game

## What goes in each case study

Context — the business problem, not just the tech ask
Your role — solo build vs. leading a team; call out scope explicitly
Stack — and why, briefly (this is where your SQS/SNS-style reasoning goes)
Architecture — one simple diagram beats three paragraphs
A key decision or trade-off — the thing a senior engineer would actually be asked about in an interview
Outcome — the metric, tied back to the business problem from step 1
What you'd do differently — optional, but this signals maturity fast
